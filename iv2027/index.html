Introduction

Foundation models, vision-language-action (VLA) models and world models are moving intelligent vehicles from modular pipelines towards end-to-end embodied intelligence. Yet impressive demonstrations are not trustworthy autonomy. On public roads, in dense cities, in the air and in mines, vehicles face distribution shift, degraded GNSS and sensing, hallucinated actions, scarce corner-case data and uncertain human intent.

A vehicle that relies on learned policies must not fail silently: it must quantify uncertainty, monitor its own integrity, expose the limits of its decisions and fall back safely. This workshop brings together researchers in foundation models and robot learning with experts in localization, perception, 3D scene reconstruction, planning, human factors and safety assurance. Our central question is:

How can embodied AI in intelligent vehicles remain robust, transparent, accountable and safety-assured in open traffic environments?

The programme combines invited talks, failure-case lightning talks, a peer-reviewed poster session, "Ask-a-senior" tables for students and early-career researchers, and a panel discussion driven by a live audience poll.

Themes and Topics

1. Foundation models, world models and scene reconstruction End-to-end driving and VLA models, multimodal scene reasoning, 3D Gaussian Splatting and NeRF for mapping and digital twins, generative simulation and data engines for corner cases.
2. Trustworthy autonomy Uncertainty quantification, out-of-distribution detection, integrity monitoring of positioning and perception, runtime verification, safe fallback, certification of learned components.
3. Human-centred and connected autonomy Intent modelling of road users, shared control and takeover, explainability to drivers and regulators, V2X and cooperative perception.
4. Deployment and evaluation Closed-loop testing in reconstructed real-world scenes, sim-to-real transfer, benchmarks and datasets, onboard compute limits, low-cost autonomy for emerging economies.

Call for Papers

We invite contributions on trustworthy embodied AI for intelligent vehicles, including ground vehicles, UAVs and other autonomous platforms. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Foundation, VLA and world models for perception, prediction, planning and control
  • 3D scene reconstruction (3DGS, NeRF), digital twins and generative simulation for testing and data generation
  • Uncertainty quantification, out-of-distribution detection and failure prediction
  • Integrity monitoring of GNSS/multi-sensor localization and perception in degraded environments
  • Runtime verification, safe fallback and certification of learned components
  • Human intent modelling, shared control, takeover and explainability
  • V2X, cooperative perception and connected autonomy
  • Closed-loop benchmarks, datasets and sim-to-real evaluation
  • Affordable and robust autonomy for emerging economies and mixed traffic
